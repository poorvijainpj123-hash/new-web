About the Atlas

भारत | Bharat Cultural Atlas is a cultural encyclopedia inspired by palm-leaf manuscripts, Mughal miniatures, and modern editorial design. Journey from region to state, discovering geography, history, festivals, art, and the rhythms of everyday life.

This demo showcases North India with deep dives into Rajasthan and Punjab. Every card, gradient, and ornament is crafted without stock photography—only pattern, color, and story.

May we see truth in diversity — एकता में अनेकता।

— Cultural Atlas motto
